HOW TO PLAY
  • Draft 11 players in a snake draft against your opponent.
  • Every down you're dealt 6 play cards: always a Run, Pass, Play-Action and Option (defense: Run Stop, Blitz, Zone, Man) plus 2 wildcards. Used plays cool down.
  • Each call beats one style and loses to another. The edge is revealed after the snap.
  • Offense: WASD / arrows / stick to move. Drag on the field and release to throw, or press 1-4 for a quick throw. SPACE = juke, E = give/pitch on options.
  • Defense: you control one defender (yellow arrow). E = switch to the defender nearest the ball. SPACE = dive / rip off a block.
  • Kicks: SPACE/tap once for power, again for accuracy.
  • Halftime: pick a team perk. Overtime is sudden death.
